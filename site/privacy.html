Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The Zoomify app

  • Zoomify uses your camera and microphone only to show the viewfinder and to record the photos and videos you take.
  • Photos and videos are saved on your phone (in DCIM/Zoomify). They are never uploaded anywhere.
  • The app has no accounts, analytics, tracking or ads, and doesn't send any data off your device.
