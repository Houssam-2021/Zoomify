Privacy policy

Your photos stay yours.

Last updated: November 2026. This policy covers the Zoomify Android app and the website at zoomify.houseli.site.

The Zoomify app

  • Camera and microphone. Zoomify uses the camera to show the viewfinder and take photos and videos, and the microphone only to record sound with videos (you can turn this off in Settings).
  • Your media stays on your phone. Photos and videos are saved to your phone's storage (DCIM/Zoomify). Zoomify has no internet permission, so it can't upload them or anything else.
  • No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking in the app.
  • Motion sensor. Horizon lock reads the phone's gravity sensor to keep videos level. This stays on the device.
  • Vibration is used for haptic feedback only.

This website

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver and protect the site. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Downloads. The APK download is served by GitHub Releases, so GitHub's privacy statement applies to the download itself.
  • Interactive demo. The demo on the home page uses an illustrated scene. It never asks for camera or microphone access.
  • Cookies and analytics. The website currently sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

Children

Zoomify isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
