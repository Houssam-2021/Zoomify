Is 50× zoom actually optical?+

No. Zoomify combines the camera's supported hardware zoom with additional software cropping and magnification. At high zoom levels, image quality depends on the sensor and can look softer.

Can every Android phone record 4K at 30 FPS?+

No. 4K/30 is an available capture option, but actual support depends on the camera, device, Android implementation and selected recording path. Zoomify may fall back to another resolution if the device doesn't support the requested combination.

What's the difference between Cinematic and Sensor HQ?+

Cinematic is designed to include zoom and creative processing in recordings. Sensor HQ prioritizes the camera's own stream and offers higher-fidelity modes such as HDR on compatible hardware. Their available effects and formats differ.

Do I need root or a Zoomify account?+

No root is needed to install the ordinary Android app. The website's download button points straight to a GitHub APK release — no Zoomify account is required.

Does the interactive demo use my camera?+

Not at all. This website contains an illustrated interactive preview. It doesn't request camera access, take photos, record videos or show your surroundings.

How do I get future updates?+

Each version is published as its own GitHub Release (starting with v1.1), and the download button always points to the newest verified one. Download it and install it over the existing app: Android keeps your photos and the app installed.

Why did Zoomify's signing key change in v1.1?+

The key used to sign Zoomify 1.0 was accidentally published, so v1.1 is signed with a new private key and carries an Android key-rotation proof. Installing v1.1 over 1.0 works as a normal update, and afterwards your phone only accepts updates signed with the new key. Only download Zoomify from this site or its GitHub page. Details.

Which phones does Zoomify work on?+

Android 11 or newer. Features such as 60 fps, telephoto, 0.5× ultra-wide, RAW, 10-bit HDR and Log depend on what your phone's camera exposes to apps, and Zoomify only shows what your phone supports. See the compatibility guide.
