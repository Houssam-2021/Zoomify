Questions

Google Play says "Item not found"

Check that your Play Store account is the same one you joined the group with (profile picture at the top right of the Play Store). After joining, wait a few minutes and try step 2 again.

I already have Zoomify from this site

Uninstall Zoomify first, then install MegaCam from Google Play. Your photos stay in your gallery.

How do I give feedback?

On MegaCam's Play Store page, use "Send private feedback to developer" (only the developer sees it). Or comment or message on TikTok @mituhype.

Does it cost anything? Are my photos sent anywhere?

No. MegaCam is free, has no internet access, and your photos stay on your phone. Privacy
