About & contact

Made by a creator, for creators.

Zoomify is an independent Android camera project by Houssam, focused on fluid zoom, cinematic motion and colour. It isn't affiliated with Samsung, Google or Apple.

Get in touch

Bugs, feature requests and questions: open an issue on GitHub. Please include your phone model and Android version.

Updates and clips: TikTok @mituhype
